Internal Review

A simulated full peer review of the draft, before the venue sees it.

A junior and a senior reviewer from the pool each work through both parts — Format (against the venue's guidelines) and Science (contributions vs. evidence). The pool is every paper's authors plus volunteers, with at least one senior. Both reviewers are assigned per submission on the Internal Review Board; the paper can't be approved until both have finished the checklist.
