Work with us

Our strength is the strength of our people. A new collaboration does not add, it multiplies.

Help us build the next AI paradigm: systems that learn, reason, coordinate and adapt in noisy, interactive, resource-constrained settings. We make two bets: paradigm-breaking discoveries are still possible, and academia, given focus, scale and compute, is still the best place to make them.

To do this, we are building a new operating system and research model for fundamental AI research in academia. Join us!
