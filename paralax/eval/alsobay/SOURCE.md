# Source of HPTConfig.json

Copied unchanged from the Group-AI Interaction Laboratory (GRAIL) platform,
https://github.com/microsoft/group_ai_lab (file `server/src/HPTConfig.json`), MIT licence,
Microsoft. It holds the hidden-profile stimuli of Alsobay, Rothschild, Hofman & Goldstein (2025),
"Bringing Everyone to the Table: An Experimental Study of LLM-Facilitated Group Decision Making",
arXiv 2508.08242: the general brief and the five personal reports (Green, Blue, Pink, Red, Orange)
for choosing a host city among Eldoron, Myloria and Cragnio.

Note: the `playerContent_fullinfo` sheets contain 24 facts; the five personal reports together
contain the paper's 30. `eval/alsobay.py` uses their union.

The study's transcripts and survey data (OSF, https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/ERVNB, CDLA v2) are
not used here; that licence excludes evaluating AI models with them.
